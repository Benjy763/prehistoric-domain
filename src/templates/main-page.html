The Experience
  • Jurassic Tour is a free VR experience
  • It's is a 20 minutes night tour in the park

System Requirements
  • You need a VR Ready PC to enjoy the experience, test here
  • You must run the game from a WebVR compatible browser
  • Firefox is recommended, verify the configuration here
  • Compatible headset: Quest, Rift S, Rift CV1, Vive, Vive Cosmos, Vive Pro, Vive Index, Windows Mixed Reality
  • You need a good internet speed
FAQ
> How to view or record the VR screen with Oculus?
You can use oculus mirror:
C:\Program Files\Oculus\Support\oculus-diagnostics\OculusMirror.exe

> How to view or record the VR screen with Steam?
You can use SteamVR -> Display VR View

> I'm not centered when I'm in VR.
Exit browser, recenter your headset, restart browser
